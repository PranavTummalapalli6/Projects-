## What Chip Counter is

Chip Counter is a notebook for friendly home poker games: it suggests chip values, times a night, records buy-ins and cash-outs, helps count chips, and works out who owes whom.

## What it is not

- It is **not a gambling service**. It deals no cards, takes no bets, and offers no prizes.
- It **doesn't handle money**. It never moves, holds or collects any. When it says someone owes someone else, that is arithmetic on numbers you typed, and settling it is between the people at the table.
- It is **not financial advice** and not a record you should rely on for tax or legal purposes.

## Your responsibilities

- Whether and how you play poker with others, and for what stakes, is for you and your own local rules and laws. You're responsible for following them.
- Check what you enter. The app's counts from a photo are **estimates** (especially from the side, and especially for tall stacks); the app asks you to confirm them, and you do.
- Keep your account secure. If you can't sign in, contact us.
